About

Allen Patnaik received the B.Tech degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from G.I.F.T. Institute, Odisha, India, in 2014, and the M.Tech degree in Communication System Engineering from Veer Surendra Sai University of Technology, Odisha, India, in 2018. He received the PhD degree in Signal Processing and Machine Learning from the Department of Electronics and Electrical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati, in 2025 (viva voce, October 2025).

His research sits at the intersection of deep learning, computer vision, and remote sensing, with a focus on image super-resolution, hyperspectral imaging, and knowledge distillation for long-term environmental monitoring.

Research focus

Super-resolution & attention architectures

Adversarial, multi-branch, and lattice-based deep networks that recover fine spatial detail in remote sensing imagery.

Hyperspectral & multispectral imaging

Grouped second-order spatial-spectral attention mechanisms that jointly preserve spatial and spectral fidelity.

Earth observation applications

Knowledge distillation for long-term NDVI reconstruction, and Landsat downscaling for land surface temperature.
