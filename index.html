Allen Patnaik received the B.Tech degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from the G.I.F.T. Institute, Odisha, India, in 2014, and the M. Tech. degree in communication system engineering from the V.S.S.U.T University, Odisha, India, in 2018. He received the Ph.D. degree in signal processing and machine learning from the EEE Deptartment, Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati, Guwahati, India, in 2025. His research focuses on deep learning, computer vision, and remote sensing, with an emphasis on image super-resolution, hyperspectral imaging, and knowledge distillation for environmental monitoring applications.
